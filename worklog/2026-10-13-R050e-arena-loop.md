## Integration decisions

Three development agents worked in isolated managed worktrees on native Git,
SQLite workflow and LiveView UI; root integrated their commits, implemented the
supervised executor/check transport, and fixed review findings. Product execution
is sequential. No runtime/provider dependency was added.

The native check grant was narrowed to native single-process system commands.
General toolchain subprocesses are unsupported; no broad home/library grants were
introduced to make Mix/npm appear supported. Check failures retain public receipts
and route through independent review; missing/failed checks cannot be waived.
Prompt preflight reserves worst-case escaped summaries/check metadata; criteria
are never truncated. Old task completion is not projected onto an edited revision.

Cancellation originally discarded native receipts and inferred success from exit0.
It now preserves bounded cleanup evidence and uncertainty, and checks authority
before launch/input. The native check supervisor must own an unreaped direct
child; PID/start lookup alone cannot establish safe signalling authority.

No account/model calls, remote Git actions or publication are part of this work.
Full real-account Arena acceptance, general toolchain checks, complete recovery,
parallel workers, custom stages and release verification remain explicit gates.
