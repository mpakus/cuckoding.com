## Intended outcome

Allocate collision-free loopback preview ports from each project's declared range, start only the trusted `dev_server` command with injected port values, expose measured health, and release/reallocate ports across stop and resume.

## Acceptance criteria restated

- Concurrent allocations cannot assign one port to two active runs.
- Allocation records a loopback-only preview URL and a durable port lease, and injects matching `PORT` and `CUCKODING_PORT` values into the declared dev-server command.
- Stop/hibernate releases the port; resume obtains a fresh currently available port rather than assuming the old one.
- Health probing distinguishes reachable, unhealthy, and unavailable previews without following redirects or accessing a non-loopback URL.
- The UI exposes preview health, a safe preview link, and non-drag worktree open actions.
- An end-to-end sample server is reachable through the allocated preview URL and is cleaned up without an orphan or leased port.
