04 / LEGAL ASKED US TO CLARIFY

The small print.
The big questions.

The dystopia is satire.
The engineering is serious.

Wait. Is this a real product?+

Yes. Cuckoding is the home of B*SS, a local coding-agent orchestrator currently in development. The robot spa is a joke. Markdown specs or committed docs, isolated Git worktrees, your checks and an independent review are the actual work. Done means that commit was published and the checks passed again.

Will it ask me every five minutes?+

That's the job we're trying to retire. New workflows send ordinary questions and failures to another model for diagnosis, then make a fresh attempt. Retries have limits, and missing access or an unresolved decision can still need you. Autonomy is the direction; “never gets stuck” would be advertising.

Am I actually surrendering control?+

You explicitly start the work and choose the check command and reviewer. Ordinary workflows wait for your integration. Autopilot needs a separate start for a saved backlog. Pause and Stop remain available. A merge conflict, unknown cleanup, an unresolved decision or a provider outage still waits for you. After an ordinary pre-integration failure, other eligible tasks can continue.

Does “local” mean the AI runs offline?+

The coordinator and dashboard run locally. Coding agents use your installed CLI tools and provider accounts, which may send data to their services and incur charges. The robot's holiday is not necessarily free.

Where can I get it?+

Follow the GitHub project for source and the local macOS beta guide. Task execution is macOS. A public installer is still ahead, and Linux execution is not available. The robots have asked us not to rush their onboarding.
