ACT I / THE PROPOSITION↓

Bread. Circuses.
Better commits.

You have a folder, a plan, and several very confident agents.

CCoding is being built to give them a process: write the spec, do the work, review the result. One local app to keep the work moving while you watch from a reasonably comfortable seat.

The ambition: set up in ten minutes. Start once. Get a working, tested, independently reviewed result.

I. Your machine. Your files.

II. A process, not a prompt pile.

III. Applause is not a passing test.

ACT II / A CIVILIZED PROCESSPlanned workflow

A little structure.
A lot less “is it done?”

An Arena is your project folder.
A Tabula is its board.
A battle is one authorized run.

  1. 01

    Assemble your agents.

    Authorize a local runtime, choose its model and save your roles once. Codex, Claude Code, Cursor and Hermes are on the roadmap.

  2. 02

    Give them something to do.

    Choose a folder. Bring a brief or documents. Turn them into specs, tasks and a Tabula with clear responsibility.

  3. 03

    Start the battle.

    Summa Rudis coordinates the work. Agents implement, review and revise. You follow progress and step in when needed.

The intended team. Give each role an agent and a model. Add your own roles when the empire requires middle management.

ISpeculatorThe one with the scroll.
Turns your brief and project documents into specs and tasks. Refines the plan when review finds holes.
IIImplementorThe one doing the fighting.
Writes the code and tests in an isolated worktree. Delivers a candidate, not a victory speech.
IIISecutorThe difficult one. Thankfully.
Checks the work against the spec and evidence. Passes it or sends comments back for another round.
IVSumma RudisThe referee with the whole picture.
Coordinates tasks, workers and handoffs. Keeps the battle moving. Cannot overrule a failed review.

This is an open-source work in progress.
Here is what actually exists.

Available in the local development build

The foundations are standing.

  • macOS menubar shell and authenticated browser UI
  • Local SQLite storage, durable commands and events
  • Detection of installed agent executables and RTK
  • Packaged launch, authentication and shutdown checks

Detection does not authorize or run an agent. The build is not a signed or notarized public release.

Next through the gates

The gladiators are in training.
