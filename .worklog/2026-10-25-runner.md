  mailboxes are not demand-controlled. Independent stdout/stderr EOF is absent.
- macOS Apple Silicon only. Linux, clean-machine release/compilation, helper
  crash, host crash, detached-tree containment, and real tool cancellation remain
  unverified. No UI, durable run model or BOSS delivery claim is made.
